## Hello, world

This is a sample post shipped with the content hub template. It exists so the site
looks complete out of the box — replace it with your own writing.

The hub is meant to aggregate content from many repositories. Each project contributes
posts and docs through a pull request, and the hub builds and deploys them for free.

## What you get

- A localized landing page and blog.
- Per-product documentation with a sidebar.
- Free deployment to GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages.
