# Welcome to the hub

This site is a **content hub**: it collects documentation and blog posts from
many repositories into one fast, localized, static website.

Content arrives through pull requests, so nothing is published to the main
branch without a human review. That keeps the site trustworthy even when dozens
of projects contribute to it.

If you are new here, the [docs](/vite/docs/) are a good place to start.
